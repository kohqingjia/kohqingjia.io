## What it is
A bilingual marketing site with a CMS, plus a read-only sales dashboard over more than 1,500 orders a month.

## What I did
Shipped the relaunch in 9 weeks by leading a workflow of Claude Code agents: parallel QA agents on isolated database copies, agent review before every merge, and a translate, critique and refine pipeline for Chinese copy. The owner edits 13 CMS sections alone, and that cut average site-update time by 80%. The dashboard turned hours of monthly Excel reporting into minutes.

## Trade-off
The dashboard deliberately uses no AI. Finance numbers must be deterministic.
